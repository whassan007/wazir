# Agent 17 — Dafny adapter (CX)

Implemented `DafnyAdapter` against the C0 `SolverAdapter` contract. No change to `packages/assurance/src/index.ts`.

## Behavior

- Runs pinned Dafny (constructor override or DAFNY_PATH supported), records actual version, SHA-256 source hash, elapsed time, workspace revision, assumptions, scope and seed.
- Runs `verify --verify-included-files --warn-contradictory-assumptions --allow-warnings`, then `audit` when available.
- Maps failing assertions/contracts/termination to COUNTEREXAMPLE with full location diagnostics; resolution/type/parse errors to ERROR; timeout to UNKNOWN; unavailable tool to NOT_RUN.
- Successful verification and a passing smoke check with no trusted gaps produce PROVED. Trusted gaps cap the result at SPECIFICATION_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Explicit false requirements and contradictory-assumptions warnings produce INCONSISTENT_ASSUMPTIONS.
- Scanner reports occurrences and line snippets for assume, axiom, extern, verify false, opaque, trusted, and bodyless declarations outside trait/abstract scopes. Includes conservatively block PROVED because their trusted base is not represented by the root-source hash. Contradiction attributes also block PROVED because they can suppress smoke diagnostics. Audit findings block PROVED.

## Limits / unexecuted checks

Vacuity detection uses Dafny's experimental contradictory-assumptions smoke check plus explicit requires-false scanning; it is not a separate complete satisfiability decision procedure or generated caller witness. Dafny documents potential spurious smoke warnings. The lexical scanner is conservative rather than a full Dafny parser and does not offer witness exemptions. Memory limits are not enforced by this subprocess adapter. Timeout applies per subprocess, not as one overall wall-clock deadline. Full repository tests were not run.
