## Summary of Changes

Implemented the canonical Group A Cross-Harness Execution Contract (`packages/core/src/types/harnessContract.ts`) and its comprehensive conformance/unit test suite (`packages/core/tests/harnessContract.test.ts`):

1. **Canonical Contract Types (`HARNESS_CONTRACT_SCHEMA_VERSION = 1`)**:
   - Defined `HarnessKind`, `HarnessCapability`, `HarnessDescriptor`, `UnifiedExecutionRequest`, `UnifiedExecutionStatus`, `UnifiedArtifactRecord`, and `UnifiedExecutionResult` in exact alignment with `docs/implementation-abc/SHARED-CONTRACTS.md`.
2. **Runtime Validation & Normalization**:
   - `validateUnifiedExecutionRequest(req)`: Validates required identity fields, workspace revision, numeric budget constraints, verification requirements, policy context, and optional cancellation hook.
   - `validateUnifiedExecutionResult(res)`: Enforces required fields, non-negative durations/token counts, valid `HarnessKind`/`UnifiedExecutionStatus`/`CostStatus`, and two critical semantic invariants:
     - **Invariant 1 (Apparent vs. Verified Completion)**: Rejects `verified: true` unless `status === 'completed'` and `verificationEvidenceIds` is non-empty.
     - **Invariant 3 (Cost Semantics)**: Rejects `costStatus === 'UNKNOWN'` when `costUSD` is defined (preventing coercion of unknown costs to `0`), and requires a finite non-negative `costUSD` when `costStatus` is `'ACTUAL'` or `'ESTIMATED'`.
   - `normalizeExecutionResult(res)`: Safely demotes `verified` to `false` when evidence or status invariants are not satisfied and strips `costUSD` when `costStatus === 'UNKNOWN'`.
   - `migrateUnifiedExecutionResult(raw)`: Upgrades legacy or unversioned execution result payloads to `schemaVersion: 1`.
3. **Deterministic Contract Digest & Durable Record Conversion**:
   - `computeRequestContractDigest(req)`: Computes a deterministic SHA-256 hex digest over canonically sorted request fields while ignoring transient callbacks (`isCancelled`).
   - `fromExecutionRecordToUnifiedResult(record, options)`: Converts Wazir's durable `ExecutionRecord` to `UnifiedExecutionResult`, filtering `VerificationEvidence` to only passing evidence at the current `workspaceRevision`, preserving artifact mutation timestamps, and inferring `'ACTUAL'` (`$0`) for local runtimes vs. `'UNKNOWN'` (`undefined`) for unpriced remote runtimes.
